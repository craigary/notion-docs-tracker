New tabs are now the standard way tabs work in the Notion desktop app for Mac and Windows. There's nothing to turn on. You get them the next time you update and restart Notion. This doesn't change Notion on the web or on your phone.

To get the new tabs, make sure your desktop app is updated to version `7.44.0` or later and that you’re on Notion version `23.13.20261116.2004 `or later. You can check for updates from Notion in the Mac menu bar, or File in the Windows menu bar. You can update your Notion version by refreshing the app. The switch happens when you restart the app, never while you're in the middle of working.

You may also need to refresh Notion’s client inside the app. On Mac, press `Command + Shift + R` to refresh the client. This only refreshes the client, and doesn't update the desktop app, so you may need to do both.

## New tabs

With new tabs, all the tabs in a window share one running copy of Notion. Here's what that means day to day:

* Switching between tabs is faster.

* The sidebar stays put instead of reloading each time you switch.

* Your open tabs keep their place, including how far you scrolled and what you had selected.

* On average, Notion uses less memory when you have multiple tabs open.

## How to get new tabs

You don't need to turn anything on. The next time you update the desktop app and restart it, the new tabs are there. Notion restarts to make the switch, and your open tabs come with you.

## What happens to your windows and tabs

A few things move around when you join, so it helps to know what to expect:

* Each window holds one workspace. If a window had pages from more than one workspace, it splits into separate windows, one per workspace.

* Your tab groups carry over. Tab groups now live inside a single window, so a group won't show up in your other windows.

* If you open a link to a page in another workspace, it opens in a different window.

* Your open tabs carry over.

## Can you go back to the old tabs?

No. New tabs are now the standard way tabs work in the desktop app, so there isn't a setting to switch back.

## Good to know

A few things to keep in mind:

* Each window holds one workspace. A link to a page in another workspace opens in a different window.

* Center Peek sits over the tab bar, so you can't click tabs while it's open. Switching tabs also closes Center Peek, along with any open pop-ups.

* You can refresh a whole window, but not a single tab on its own.

* New tabs work in the Mac and Windows desktop app. Notion on the web and on your phone stay the same.

* AI Meeting Notes on desktop can have trouble with the recording indicator, with pop-up and floating windows, and with ending a recording cleanly if the app restarts.

* Some full-screen views may hide the tab bar.


## FAQs

### Will I lose my tabs?

No. Your open tabs and tab groups carry over.


### Can I switch back to the old tabs?

No. New tabs are the standard way tabs work in the desktop app now.


### Why did my window split into two?

Each window holds one workspace. If a window had pages from more than one workspace, it splits so each workspace gets its own window.


### Does this change Notion on the web or on my phone?

No. The beta is only for the Mac and Windows desktop app.
