By default, only the person who created a Worker can edit it or add it as a connection. Share a Worker so teammates can connect it to a Custom Agent or help manage it.

Available on Business and Enterprise plans.

## Sharing permissions

When you share a Worker, you can choose one of two permission levels:

* **Can Connect:** Connect the Worker to Custom Agents and view basic details.

* **Full Access:** Connect, edit, redeploy, and manage the Worker.

## How to share a Worker

1. Turn on [Developer Mode](https://www.notion.com/help/turn-on-developer-mode-to-use-developer-tools-in-notion) from `Settings`, then open Workers from the developer section in your sidebar.

2. Open the Worker you want to share.

3. Click `Share` and search for the person you want to add.

4. Choose a permission level, then click `Send invite`.

They’ll get a notification and can connect the Worker to Custom Agents. With full access, they can also manage syncs and webhooks.

## What sharing can't do yet

* **No group sharing.&#x20;**&#x59;ou can only share with individual people, not Notion groups or teams.

* **Workers without an owner.&#x20;**&#x49;f the person who created a Worker leaves your workspace, the Worker may lose its connection. If this happens, contact a workspace admin to delete the Worker.
