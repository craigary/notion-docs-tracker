We offer Business Plan trials to teams getting started on Notion.<!-- --> <!-- -->If you're new to Notion, you can start a Business Plan trial when you create your workspace.

When you start a trial from the Free Plan, you'll usually be asked to add a payment method. Most trials run for 14 or 30 days, depending on the offer. When your trial ends, your workspace moves to the paid Business Plan automatically unless you cancel before then. If there's no payment method on file when your trial ends, you'll be asked to confirm the upgrade instead. You can see how many days are left in your trial, and cancel it anytime, from `Settings` → `Billing`.

## What is the Business Plan trial?

During a Business Plan trial, you’ll have access to all features in the Business Plan, which include:

* Notion AI, including AI Meeting Notes, Research Mode, Notion AI connectors, and more

* Verified pages

* Private teamspaces

* SAML SSO

* Premium connections

For a comprehensive list of what features come with the Business Plan, refer to our [pricing page →](https://www.notion.com/pricing)

## How do I know if my team is eligible?

Business Plan trials are available only to teams who use a professional domain and aren’t already on a Business or Enterprise Plan. If you’re using an educational domain or a personal domain, your team won’t be eligible and will need to set up a new workspace using a professional domain to get access to the offer.

You’ll also need to be using Notion’s web or desktop app to start the trial.

## Upgrade your trial early

You can upgrade at any time during your trial:

1. Go to `Settings` → `Billing`.

2. Select `Manage trial`.

3. Choose to upgrade.

If you haven't added a payment method yet, you'll be asked to enter one. Your paid plan starts as soon as you confirm.

If your trial is already set to continue on the Business Plan, `Manage trial` shows the date and estimated amount you'll be charged instead of an upgrade option. You don't need to do anything to keep Business.

## Cancel your trial

You can cancel your trial anytime. If your trial is running on top of a plan you already pay for, it ends right away and you go back to that plan. If you started your trial on the Free Plan, you’ll keep Business Plan features until your trial ends, then move to the Free Plan. You'll also lose access to features that only come with the Business Plan.

Learn more about what happens when you [downgrade your plan →](https://www.notion.com/help/plan-downgrade)

You'll need to be a workspace owner to see billing settings. To cancel your trial:

1. Go to `Settings` → `Billing`.

2. Next to **Business Trial**, select `Manage trial`.

3. Select `Cancel trial`.

4. Review the features you'll lose access to, then confirm your cancellation.

If you see a banner about a scheduled plan change, your cancellation is already set. You can undo it from that banner. If you’re having trouble cancelling your trial, you can [contact support](https://www.notion.com/help) for help.

If you don't see `Billing` in Settings, ask a workspace owner to cancel the trial.

## What happens at the end of my trial?

If your workspace has a payment method on file, your Business Plan subscription will start automatically at the end of your trial.

This also applies to most trials started while on the Plus Plan. When your trial ends, your workspace will automatically continue on the Business Plan. You agree to this before your trial starts, so there are no surprise charges. If you don’t want to continue, cancel before your trial ends.

If there's no payment method on file, you'll be prompted to confirm your upgrade to the Business Plan at the end of your trial period.
